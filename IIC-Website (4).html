The Founder's Story

I lived this reality.
That's why I built this.

Internships are meant to prepare students for the workforce. But too often, tasks drift from job descriptions, mentoring fades, and meaningful feedback is rare.

As an International Business co-op student, I experienced both sides — deeply engaging placements that shaped who I became professionally, and ones that left me feeling invisible. Useful on paper. Valueless in practice.

That contrast was the moment. Not a frustration — a realisation. The problem wasn't the students. It wasn't even the companies. It was the absence of design. Nobody had built the system. Nobody had trained the managers. Nobody had prepared the students. Everyone was improvising and calling it an internship.

Internships weren't broken — they were just underdesigned.

Driven by a passion for teaching, entrepreneurship, and building real career pathways, I founded Internship Insights Co. during my final year of university — after testing the idea with my professor, my life coach, mentors, and the people whose judgment I trust most.

Today, we work with companies, managers, and students across Bermuda to close the gap between education and employment — through structured diagnostics, skill-building programmes, and guaranteed paid placements.
